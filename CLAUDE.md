# tv

Debloat of the living-room Google TV ("Hollywood"), a custom launcher for it, and an iPhone remote.
Docs are in Spanish (Cristian reads them); code, comments and commit messages are in English.

## Layout

- `apps/tv/`: the Android TV launcher, named **Hollywood** like the TV. Kotlin, Compose + `androidx.tv:tv-material`, Jellyfin SDK, Coil.
  Package `dev.cardozo.hollywood.launcher`. Gradle project of its own (`apps/tv/gradlew`).
- `apps/ios/`: iPhone remote (`HollywoodRemote/`) and Mac menu-bar remote (`HollywoodMenu/`), sharing
  `Shared/` and `RemoteKit/`. SwiftUI, project generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`
  (the `.xcodeproj` is not committed).
- `debloat/`: package lists. `hollywood.txt` (phase A) never touches Home; `hollywood-launcher.txt`
  (phase B) hands Home to our launcher.
- `tools/tv`: adb against the TV through Ginebra. `tools/debloat.sh`: status / apply / restore.
- `design/icono/`: the «H▶» icon generator (same family as Ginebra's «G▶»; copied approach, never edit ginebra/).
- `docs/`: design docs and mockups. `baseline/`: read-only dumps of the TV (2026-10-22), local only and
  gitignored: they hold personal data (notifications, settings). The repo is public.

## The TV

- FFALCON 75" U65 (TCL G10), Google TV on Android 12 (SDK 31), 32-bit only, 1.8 GB RAM. Details in
  `docs/tele.md`.
- **Never change anything on the TV without Cristian's explicit go-ahead for that step.** People
  watch it; ask first even for "harmless" commands that change state. Read-only commands are fine.
- adb only works from Ginebra (`ssh ginebra`), never from the Mac: use `tools/tv`.
- Never disable `com.google.android.tv.remote.service` (the iOS app needs it) or anything in the
  "NEVER DISABLE" block of `debloat/hollywood.txt`.

## Building

- Everything builds locally. No CI workflows for this repo (macOS runners cost money).
- Launcher: `make tv-build`, `make tv-run` against the `tv-gtv-31` emulator (Google TV, API 31).
  The TV gets the release build: `make tv-install`. Installing kills Home and Android brings the previous
  task (e.g. YouTube) to the front: press Home afterwards only with Cristian's OK.
- Home is GPU-bound on the TV (PowerVR BXE-4-32): keep full-screen layers cached, no Modifier.alpha on
  cards (offscreen buffer each), no per-frame animations while idle. Measure with `dumpsys gfxinfo … framestats`.
  Gradle runs on Android Studio's JDK (`JAVA_HOME` is set in the Makefile).
- iOS: `make ios-build` for the simulator. Free Apple account: re-sign on the phone every 7 days.
- Mac: `make mac-build`, `make mac-run`.

## Conventions

- Keep the launcher light: no background services, images requested at card size, small Coil cache.
  The TV shares 1.8 GB with the streaming apps.
- Jellyfin playback goes to Wholphin through its `com.github.damontecres.wholphin.PLAYBACK` action with
  `itemId` / `position` extras (an ACTION_VIEW `wholphin://play` only opens the details page). The player
  is a choice in `jellyfin/Player.kt`.
